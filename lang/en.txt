563	Why no fixed focus value per filter?
564	A filter in front of the lens practically does not shift the focus. The focus position depends on the individual device and the temperature. The problem is the Seestar autofocus: it exposes briefly and sees too few stars behind dark filters. The app autofocus exposes longer, averages several images and remembers the result per device and filter.
